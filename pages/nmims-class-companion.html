NMIMS Class Companion

Built independently for my MBA batch. Sole product owner and builder.

Problem
Timetables and menus arrive as Excel sheets by email, placement news on WhatsApp, and finding a shared free slot meant comparing schedules by hand.
I built
A mobile web app with the menu, timetable, faculty availability, exams, a free-together finder and a suggestion box.
Evidence
147 different students opened their timetable between 3 August and 13 October 2026. 123 of them (84%) did so on 10 or more different days, and 137 in the last week. The app was opened 16,795 times in 72 days, about 233 a day, with 1,943 opens in the last week. Observed
Next
Interview classmates, add a privacy note about the sign-in log, and track which tabs get used.

Decisions

  • One question per tab: Food Menu, Timetable, Faculty Timetable, Classmates. Date ranges sit right under them because most checks are about now.
  • Personalize with a search, not an account. A student types a name or roll number. No sign-up.
  • Show how fresh the data is with a last-updated stamp.
  • Solve coordination directly: “Free Together” shows when a group is free at the same time.

Limits

  • The figures come from my own sign-in log, not a product analytics tool. They count student roll numbers, not devices, and I cannot see how long anyone spent in the app. Opens may include my own visits, and many people use the Food Menu without ever opening a timetable.
  • The log records name, roll number, time and browser. Students should be told that clearly.
  • The app is only as current as the sheets and messages it is fed. I did no user research before building.
