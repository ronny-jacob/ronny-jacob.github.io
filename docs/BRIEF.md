# Brief

## Goal
Make a recruiter believe within 60 seconds that Ronny is a credible candidate for Associate Product Manager and AI Product Manager roles, and make the strongest evidence impossible to miss. The site cannot prove he is "the best"; it must be credible, honest and easy to shortlist.

## The 10 / 30 / 60 second flow
- **10 s:** headline states the outcome ("took a B2B marketplace's keyword matching from 43% to 86% accuracy, and it shipped"), then who he is and what he wants.
- **30 s:** one big TradeIndia card with the before/after bars, then three smaller cards for the apps he built (NMIMS Class Companion, Invisalign Tracker, Lean 12), each with a live interactive preview.
- **60 s:** a case study opens with a four-line summary (Problem, I owned, Result, Next), then one signature visual, then Decisions and Limits.

## Positioning
He combines business understanding, analytical problem-solving and hands-on execution. Evidence over claims. Not a generic developer portfolio.

## Content model
- TradeIndia (professional): the strongest evidence. Product internship, April to June 2026.
- NMIMS Class Companion, Invisalign Tracker: independently built, live.
- Lean 12: personal tool, live, one user, no outcome claims.
- About: a three-step path (advocate 2023–25, MBA 2025–27, product intern 2026). Contact: email, LinkedIn, GitHub.

## Facts and their status
See `CLAIMS.md`. Verified facts, sources and open confirmations live there. Do not restate numbers from memory; copy them from the page or the ledger.

## Voice
Direct, specific, sentence case, plain verbs. No "passionate", "innovative", "disruptive" or "building the future".
